// capabilities

One agent. Four jobs.

Replaces a handful of point tools and most of an SDR’s research time.

Account discovery

Continuously scans your TAM and surfaces fresh ICP-fit accounts as they appear — new funding, hiring, expansions, M&A.

Intent signals

Pulls signal across web, social, job-boards, news, podcasts, GitHub, review sites. Ranks accounts by likely-to-buy this quarter.

Personalised outreach

Writes the message a human would write — tied to a real trigger event, not a token replacement. Email, LinkedIn, voice.

Meeting orchestration

Books the meeting on your AE’s calendar, writes the prep brief, syncs to your CRM, Slacks the seller. Handle the call — we’ll do the rest.
